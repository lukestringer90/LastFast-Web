Tracking your fasts should be as effortless as your daily routine. From initiating a fast to reviewing your progress, LastFast keeps you informed and motivated every step of the way, ensuring your journey is always synchronised and personalised.

Seamless Fasting Control

Take complete control with a precise timer that works even in the background. Set goal durations or specific end times, and stay on schedule with timely notifications that keep you informed without interruption.

Progress & Management

View your history through insightful graphs and chronological lists. Effortlessly manage your records by editing start times, end times, or goals, giving you full control over your data history.

Glanceable Insights

Monitor your progress instantly with sleek widgets and Live Activities on your Lock Screen. The interface supports both Dark and Light Modes, complementing your device's aesthetic perfectly.

Integration & Automation

Your data stays perfectly in sync across all Apple devices via iCloud. Enhance efficiency with Siri and Shortcuts support to build personalised workflows for your schedule.
